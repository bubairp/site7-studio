# Release Notes for Site7 Studio

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-06

The first release.

### Install a whole website
- Install a Starter Kit on a fresh Craft 5 site from **Site7 Studio → Install**: its Theme (structure, templates, design, plugins, settings), every page with its blocks, menus and demo content, downloaded from the Site7 Library.
- Every download is signed by Commerce24 and verified before anything is installed.
- **Check** shows what will be downloaded and installed, and anything that stops the install, before you start.
- Works without npm or Node on the server: the Theme brings its built design.
- Works on any Craft 5 release.

### Build pages
- **Add Section** opens the Site7 Content Browser in the page editor: insert a single block, or a whole page template.
- Templates of general pages (Home, About, Contact…) insert with their content; templates of detail pages (a blog post, a product…) insert their layout and styles, ready for your own text and images.

### Library and packages
- Install, enable, disable and remove blocks and pages from the Library. Remove deletes what a block created on your site and keeps the package in your Library to install again.
- Install anything your plan includes from **Account & License → Packages**, including packages you deleted.

### Updates
- **Updates** lists newer versions of everything installed from the Library. Updating keeps every change you made: only blocks, pages, templates and settings you haven't changed are updated. The database is backed up first.

### Account & License
- Plan, licence, subscription and account details from Commerce24.
- Your plan sets which Starter Kits you get and how many packages you can add beyond your kit (**Extra Packages**).
